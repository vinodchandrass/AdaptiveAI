\subsection{Research Questions and Contributions}

The review is based on a fixed bibliographic corpus
of 1,379 records retrieved from Web of Science.
Five research questions guide its investigation:

\begin{description}
	\item[\textbf{RQ1:}]
	What adaptation mechanisms and evaluation protocols
	are employed in open-world AI systems?
	
	\item[\textbf{RQ2:}]
	How robust is the methodological evidence supporting
	reported adaptation benefits?
	
	\item[\textbf{RQ3:}]
	To what extent does adaptation improve performance
	relative to matched static or non-adaptive baselines?
	
	\item[\textbf{RQ4:}]
	How effectively do adaptation mechanisms preserve
	previously acquired capabilities under evolving
	operational conditions?
	
	\item[\textbf{RQ5:}]
	What computational and resource trade-offs influence
	the net benefit of adaptation?
\end{description}
