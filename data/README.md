- `corpus_status.csv` — original 1,379 records, historical advancement category, **no final full-text exclusions**.
- `candidate_status.csv` — 263 preliminary candidates, all final eligibility undecided.
- `cityspec_contrasts.csv` — 48 within-study comparisons, **not 48 independent studies**.

These CSVs are now stored in the private repository. Remote record counts, identifier linkage and selected contrast arithmetic were checked on 2026-11-25. Their provenance, redistribution permissions, source-table matches and reviewer privacy require confirmation before publication.

Do not fill missing eligibility statuses with exclusions or promote preliminary values to verified final review results.
